# Figure 6 caption

**Fig. 6 | Regional data center shares of electricity and freshwater in
2050.** **a**–**c**, Data center shares of each region's electricity
consumption (**a**), freshwater consumption (**b**) and freshwater withdrawal
(**c**) under High demand at Medium efficiency in the reference
power system. Maps retain the 32 GCAM regions. Water-map color scales are
capped at 30% for consumption and 20% for withdrawal; the colorbar overflow
indicates the EFTA value above each cap. **d**–**f**, The same shares for 12
reporting regions across all 18 demand-by-efficiency-by-policy
scenarios. Grey bars span the full scenario range. Warm upward ticks show the
three demand scenarios at Medium efficiency under the
reference power system, teal downward ticks show net-zero CO$_2$ by 2050, and
grey diamonds mark calibrated 2025 shares. Panel d extends to 80%; panels e
and f extend to 50%, with 10-percentage-point ticks throughout.

Data center electricity equals AI plus conventional-compute electricity.
Freshwater footprints equal direct facility use plus indirect
power-generation use. Consumption and withdrawal remain distinct measures.
All numerators and denominators are matched by scenario, policy, GCAM region
and year before aggregation. The model reports volumetric water use and does
not represent basin-level scarcity. Map geometry uses the frozen Natural
Earth 1:110m Admin 0 country boundaries and the GCAM country-to-region map.
Source: GCAM 9.1 calibrated BaseX scenario databases queried on 30 August
2026.
