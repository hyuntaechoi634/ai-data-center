# Figure 6 caption

**Fig. 6 | Regional data center shares of electricity and water in 2050.**
**a**–**c**, Data center shares of regional electricity consumption (**a**),
water consumption (**b**) and water withdrawal (**c**) under High demand at
Medium efficiency in the reference power system. Maps retain the 32 GCAM
regions. **d**–**f**, The same shares for 12 reporting regions across all 18
demand-by-efficiency-by-policy scenarios. Grey bars span the full scenario
range. Warm upward ticks show the three demand assumptions at Medium
efficiency under the reference power system, teal downward ticks show
net-zero CO₂ by 2050, and grey diamonds mark calibrated 2025 shares.
Regional water footprints and denominators are matched by scenario, policy
and year. The model reports volumetric water use and does not represent
basin-level scarcity.

Generation: GCAM 9.1 final calibrated results (2026-08-29). Map panels retain
the 32 model regions, while lower comparison rails aggregate to 12 reporting
regions.
