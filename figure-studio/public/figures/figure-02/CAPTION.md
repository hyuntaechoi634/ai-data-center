**Fig. 2 | Power-system response to data center growth.** **a**,**b**,
Observed global gross capacity additions from 2011 to 2024 followed by
positive annualized net changes in modeled generation-equivalent capacity
under the reference power system (**a**) and net-zero CO₂ by 2050 (**b**).
Bars show High demand at Medium efficiency, whiskers span the Low and High
efficiency assumptions, and the dark line shows total clean capacity on the
right axis. **c**, Total generation-equivalent clean capacity in 2050 for the
nine demand-by-efficiency scenarios under each policy, stacked by technology.
The dashed line marks 5,529 GW of observed renewable and nuclear capacity in
2025. **d**, Electricity generation in 2050 at Medium efficiency, with
whiskers spanning the three efficiency assumptions. **e**, Electricity
consumption by end-use sector in 2050. Data centers comprise conventional,
training and inference electricity, and buildings exclude data center
consumption. Modeled capacity is inferred from generation and declared
capacity factors and is not a queried gross-build series.
