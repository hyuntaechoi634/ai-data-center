**Calibration and demand pathways by computing service.** Points and dark segments show the calibrated paths of the additional demand index from 2021 to 2025. Colored lines show the Low, Medium and High pathways after 2025. Dashed lines show the counterfactual without additional AI demand growth for training and inference. The shaded interval marks the calibration period. Vertical axes are logarithmic. Each service is normalized separately to one in 2025, so values cannot be compared or added across services.
