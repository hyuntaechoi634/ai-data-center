**Calibration and efficiency pathways by computing service.** Points and dark segments show reconstructed effective information technology efficiency from 2021 to 2025. Colored lines show the Low, Medium and High pathways after 2025 for **a**, AI training, **b**, AI inference and **c**, conventional computing. Realized performance factors are fixed after 2025, so relative changes follow rated efficiency. The shaded interval marks the calibration period. Training and inference share a vertical scale. Power usage effectiveness is excluded. Each service uses a different RFLOP definition, so levels cannot be compared or added across panels.
