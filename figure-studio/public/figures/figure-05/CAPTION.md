# Figure 5 caption

**Fig. 5 | Carbon prices and electricity-price effects of data center
growth.** **a**, Carbon-price pathways under net-zero CO$_2$ by 2050. Lines
show Medium efficiency and shaded bands span the efficiency assumptions.
**b**, Additional carbon prices relative to the matching post-2025
autonomous-intensity counterfactual at the same efficiency. Bars show Medium
efficiency and capped whiskers span the efficiency assumptions. **c**, Global
industrial electricity-price increases attributable to data center growth
under the reference and net-zero CO$_2$ by 2050 power systems. Bars show
Medium efficiency and capped whiskers span the efficiency assumptions. The
global index uses fixed regional industrial-electricity weights from the
matching policy-and-efficiency counterfactual. **d**, Regional percentage
increases in industrial electricity prices under net-zero CO$_2$ by 2050 at
Medium efficiency. The 32 model regions are aggregated to 12 reporting
regions using the same fixed counterfactual weights.

Delivered industrial electricity prices are queried from the
`elect_td_ind` market in 1975 USD GJ$^{-1}$ and converted to 2020 USD
MWh$^{-1}$ using a US-CPI factor of 4.81 and the GJ-to-MWh factor of 3.6.
Global CO$_2$ prices are queried in 1990 USD tC$^{-1}$ and converted to 2020
USD tCO$_2^{-1}$ using a factor of 0.540307. Source: GCAM 9.1 calibrated BaseX
scenario databases queried on 30 August 2026.
