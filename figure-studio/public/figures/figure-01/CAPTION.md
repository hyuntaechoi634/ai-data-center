**Fig. 1 | Compute-service growth and data center electricity consumption.**
**a**, Global training, inference and conventional compute-service volumes
from 2021 to 2050, in service-specific RFLOP units, for the three demand
assumptions. Bold lines show Medium efficiency and thin lines show Low and
High efficiency. **b**, Data center electricity consumption under the
reference power system, decomposed into conventional, training and inference
electricity at Medium efficiency. Dark lines show total consumption for each
efficiency assumption on a common vertical scale. **c**,**d**, Data center
electricity consumption (**c**) and AI electricity consumption (**d**)
compared with published outlooks. Teal lines show the nine reference
scenarios and grey lines show published trajectories. **e**, Largest annual
percentage difference in total data center electricity consumption between
net-zero CO₂ by 2050 and the reference power system for each
demand-by-efficiency scenario. Training, inference and conventional computing
are separate services and their service volumes are not added.
