# Figure 4 caption

**Fig. 4 | Global data center water use.** **a**, Water consumption, return
flow and total withdrawal from 2021 to 2050 under the Reference pathway at
Medium efficiency for the Low, Medium and High demand scenarios. The upper
edge gives total withdrawal, which equals consumption plus return flow.
**b**, Water consumption in 2050, decomposed into direct facility cooling and
indirect power-generation water at Medium efficiency. Whiskers span the three
efficiency pathways. **c**, Return flow in 2050 with the same decomposition
and scenario organization. Consumption in **b** plus return flow in **c**
equals total withdrawal in **a**. Ref. denotes Reference and NZ denotes
net-zero CO$_2$ by 2050. Volumes are annual flows, and the model does not
represent basin-level water scarcity.
