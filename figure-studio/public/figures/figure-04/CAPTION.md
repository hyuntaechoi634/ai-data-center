# Figure 4 caption

**Fig. 4 | Global data center freshwater use.** **a**, Global annual
freshwater consumption and withdrawal from 2021 to 2050 under the reference
power system at Medium efficiency for the three autonomous-intensity
assumptions. **b**, Freshwater consumption in 2050, decomposed into direct
facility use and indirect power-generation use at Medium efficiency. Whiskers
span the three efficiency assumptions. **c**, Freshwater withdrawal in 2050
with the same decomposition and scenario organization. Ref. denotes the
reference power system and NZ denotes net-zero CO$_2$ by 2050.

Direct use is calculated from the modeled data center electricity load using
1.8 L kWh$^{-1}$ for consumption and 2.25 L kWh$^{-1}$ for withdrawal.
Indirect use applies the corresponding GCAM electricity-sector freshwater
intensity in the same scenario, policy, region and year. Consumption and
withdrawal are distinct GCAM freshwater input categories and are therefore
shown independently; consumption is not subtracted from withdrawal.
Power-sector consumption includes reservoir evaporation, while seawater is
excluded. Volumes are annual flows, and the model does not represent
basin-level water scarcity. Source: GCAM 9.1 calibrated BaseX scenario
databases queried on 30 August 2026.
