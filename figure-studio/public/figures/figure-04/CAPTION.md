# Figure 4 caption

**Fig. 4 | Global data center water use.** **a**, Water consumption, return
flow and total withdrawal from 2021 to 2050 under the reference power system
at Medium efficiency for the three demand assumptions. The top edge gives
total withdrawal, which equals consumption plus return flow. **b**, Water
consumption in 2050, decomposed into direct facility cooling and indirect
power-generation water at Medium efficiency. Whiskers span the three
efficiency assumptions. **c**, Return flow in 2050 with the same scenario
organization. Consumption in **b** plus return flow in **c** equals total
withdrawal in **a**. Volumes are annual flows, and the model does not
represent basin-level water scarcity.

Generation: GCAM 9.1 final calibrated results (2026-08-29).
Electricity-water intensities and total regional water denominators are
matched by scenario, policy, region and year.
