**Historical global electricity generation and capacity additions.** **a**, Global electricity generation by source from 2000 to 2025. **b**, Shares supplied by renewable and clean sources over the same period. **c**, Tracked gross nameplate capacity commissioned from 2000 to 2024, grouped by unit start year. Panels a and b use reported and estimated annual generation from Ember's *Global Electricity Review 2026*. Panel c uses the August 2026 Global Integrated Power Tracker. The tracker applies technology-specific size thresholds and does not comprehensively cover distributed solar, so panel c is a tracked utility-scale gross series rather than a complete global total.
