# Figure 3 caption

**Fig. 3 | Data center CO₂ emissions and remaining carbon budgets.**
**a**, Annual indirect emissions from data center electricity consumption,
calculated with region-specific gross grid intensities and summed globally.
Lines show Medium efficiency for each demand assumption under the reference
power system and net-zero CO₂ by 2050, while shading spans the Low and High
efficiency assumptions. **b**, Cumulative indirect emissions from 2026 to
2050 under the reference power system, decomposed into AI training, AI
inference and conventional computing. Solid and hatched additions show an
illustrative manufacturing sensitivity at 15% and up to 40% of cumulative
indirect emissions, informed by published life-cycle inventories and
reported as a CO₂e-informed addition on the GtCO₂ scale. The sensitivity is
not a definitive addition to the operational CO₂ inventory, and its
comparison with the CO₂-only budgets is illustrative.
Horizontal lines mark the remaining 1.5 °C budgets from 2025. **c**,
Global-average gross emission intensity of electricity, showing the model's
own 2005–2025 history and each policy from 2025, against trajectories from
the IPCC AR6 Scenarios Database.
Bioenergy with carbon capture and storage removals are not credited to data
center electricity consumption.

Generated from GCAM 9.1 final calibrated results on 2026-08-29. Panels **a**
and **b** use regional production-weighted attribution. Panel **c** is a
separate global-average grid indicator.
