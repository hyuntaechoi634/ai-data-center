# Figure 3 caption

**Fig. 3 | Data center CO₂ emissions and remaining carbon budgets.**
**a**, Annual indirect emissions from data center electricity consumption,
calculated with region-specific gross grid intensities and summed globally.
Lines show Medium efficiency for each demand assumption under the reference
power system and net-zero CO₂ by 2050, while shading spans the Low and High
efficiency assumptions. **b**, Cumulative indirect emissions under the
reference power system, calculated by continuous trapezoidal integration from
2026.0 to 2050.0 and decomposed into AI training, AI inference and
conventional computing. Solid and hatched additions show an illustrative
manufacturing sensitivity at 15% and up to 40% of cumulative indirect
emissions. The range is informed by published server life-cycle inventories
and accelerator product carbon-footprint reports [aslan2024; nvidia2025]; it
is not an empirically estimated uncertainty interval or a definitive addition
to the operational CO₂ inventory. Its comparison with the CO₂-only budgets is
illustrative. Horizontal lines mark the remaining carbon budgets from the
start of 2026 [forster2026]. **c**, Global-average gross emission intensity of
electricity from the canonical BaseX electricity-generation query. The model
history is common across scenarios through 2025; each policy line after 2025
is the unweighted mean across the nine core demand-efficiency combinations.
Thin lines show trajectories from the IPCC AR6 Scenarios Database [byers2022].
GCAM intensities do not credit bioenergy with carbon capture and storage
removals; the AR6 trajectories retain their native database accounting.

Generated from the calibrated GCAM 9.1 24-scenario database archive. Panels
**a** and **b** use regional production-weighted attribution. Panel **c** is a
separate global-average grid indicator.
